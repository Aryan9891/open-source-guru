---
title: Goals
description: Targets for your Open Source Contributions, every week, every month or once.
---

Set goals on the [Goals](/dashboard/goals) page, such as "30 Open Source Contributions a month" or
"1 review a week". They count [Open Source Contributions](/docs/contributions) only.

## What to count

| Metric | Counts |
|---|---|
| Open Source Contributions | Everything, commits counted one by one |
| Active days | Days with at least one contribution |
| Commits, Issues opened, Pull requests opened, Reviews, Discussions | One type |
| Pull requests merged | Your pull requests merged in the period, whenever they were opened |

## When

- **Every week** (Sunday to Saturday) or **every month**: starts with the current week or month.
  The last 12 periods are kept, so you can see how often you reached it.
- **Once, between two dates**: for example "100 contributions by December 31".

## Where

- **All open-source repositories** (default).
- **Repositories you follow**: the ones you have [subscriptions](/docs/subscriptions) for.
- **One repository**, for example a project you want to get involved in.

## Progress

Progress is updated after each refresh of your contributions (at least every 6 hours) and as
soon as you set or change a goal. Once a period reaches its target, it stays **achieved**, even
if a later recount lowers it a bit.

Turn on **Goal achieved** in [Settings](/dashboard/settings) under **Motivation emails** to get a
congratulation email when you reach a goal.

## Public goals

Goals are private unless you make them public: then they appear on your
[public profile](/docs/profile) with their current progress. Change it with **Make public** or
**Make private** on each goal.

You can have up to 20 goals. **Archive** a goal to stop tracking it while keeping its history;
**Delete** removes it with its history.
