---
title: Getting started
description: Sign in, set up your emails and follow your first repository.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sign in with GitHub

Click **Sign in** and authorize Open Source Guru on GitHub. It's the only way to sign in:
contributing to GitHub projects needs a GitHub account anyway. No account yet?
[Create one on GitHub](https://github.com/signup).

We only ask for your email addresses (read-only). Everything else we use is public.

</Step>
<Step>

### Set up your emails

The first time you sign in, a short setup asks for:

- **Your time zone**, detected from your browser.
- **The hour for daily digests**, if you choose daily emails for a subscription.
- **Your public profile**: on by default at `/u/<your login>`; you can turn it off right there.
  See [Public profile](/docs/profile).

Notifications go to your primary GitHub email address. You can
[add other addresses](/docs/emails#email-addresses) later.

</Step>
<Step>

### Follow a repository

Type a repository (`owner/name` or its GitHub URL) and pick labels from the list. Each label shows
how many open issues have it. Then choose how often you want emails:

| Frequency | You get |
|---|---|
| Right away | An email within a few minutes of a match |
| Hourly | One email per hour with everything that matched |
| Daily | One email per day, at the hour you chose |

<Callout>
  Tried the form on the home page before signing in? Your repository and labels are kept, and the
  subscription is ready when you arrive.
</Callout>

</Step>
<Step>

### Check your feed

Your [dashboard](/dashboard) lists every issue that matched your subscriptions, newest first. For
each one you see its labels and whether it's **not assigned yet**, **assigned** to someone, or
**closed**.

Only issues detected **after** you subscribed show up: following a repository doesn't replay its
past issues. See [How it works](/docs/how-it-works).

</Step>
<Step>

### Watch your contributions grow

While you set things up, we fetch your
[Open Source Contributions](/docs/contributions) from GitHub. Your
[Contributions](/dashboard/contributions) page shows them, with your [streak](/docs/streaks);
set [goals](/docs/goals) to keep going.

</Step>
</Steps>
