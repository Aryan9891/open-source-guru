## Your public profile

Your profile is at `/u/<your GitHub login>`. It shows:

- your GitHub avatar, name and bio, with a link to your GitHub profile,
- your Open Source Contributions graph and total, with the year picker,
- your current and longest streak,
- the goals you made public,
- your contributions by type and by repository, with licenses and links to GitHub, and your
  recently merged pull requests.

It **never** shows your email addresses, your subscriptions, your settings or what wasn't counted.

Shared links get a preview card with your avatar, your total of the last 12 months and your
current streak.

## Turning it off

Profiles are **public by default**. To turn yours off, use the **Public profile** switch in
[Settings](/dashboard/settings) under **Profile** (or during sign-up). It takes effect
immediately: the page shows "not found" from then on. Turn it back on at any time.

## README badge

Add your Open Source Contributions to your GitHub profile README or a project's README:

```md
[![Open Source Contributions](https://<this site>/u/<login>/badge.svg)](https://<this site>/u/<login>)
```

The badge shows your Open Source Contributions of the last 12 months and your current streak, for
example **Open Source Guru | 94 contributions · 5-week streak**, and links to your profile. Copy
the exact Markdown from [Settings](/dashboard/settings) under **Profile**, or from your own
profile page.

The badge is updated every hour. When your profile is off, it shows **not available**.

### Customizing color and size

Customize the badge appearance with query parameters:

- `color`: Background color for the stats side (hex code like `?color=45056E` or CSS name like `?color=blue`).
- `size`: Badge size preset (`small` [default], `medium`, `large`) or numeric pixel height (e.g. `?size=28`).

```md
[![Open Source Contributions](https://<this site>/u/<login>/badge.svg?color=45056E&size=medium)](https://<this site>/u/<login>)
```
