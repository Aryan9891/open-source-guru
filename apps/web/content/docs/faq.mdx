## Is it free?

Yes. Open Source Guru is free and open source, and built to run on a small budget.

## Why sign in with GitHub only?

Contributing to open-source projects on GitHub needs a GitHub account anyway, and it means one less
password for you. We only ask for read access to your email addresses.

## Why didn't I get an email for an issue?

A few common reasons:

- **It was opened before you subscribed.** Subscriptions don't replay past issues.
- **It didn't have your labels when it was opened**, and **Labels added to existing issues** is off
  in that subscription.
- **"All of these labels" is on** and the issue doesn't have every label.
- **The subscription is paused**, or its email address is **disabled** (bounced or unsubscribed).
- **It's a pull request.** Only issues are followed.
- **It's on its way.** Emails sent right away can take 5 to 10 minutes, and digests wait for the
  next hour or your digest hour. See [How it works](/docs/how-it-works#how-long-it-takes).

Your [feed](/dashboard) lists everything that matched, even if an email hasn't been sent yet.

## Can I follow private repositories?

No. Only public repositories, since the goal is open-source contribution.

## Can I follow a label in every repository?

Not yet: a subscription is for one repository. You can have up to 50.

## Can I get notifications on Discord, Slack or elsewhere?

Not yet. Discord, Slack, webhooks and [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) are planned. In the meantime, the
[API](/docs/api) lets you read your feed from your own tools.

## I found a bug or have an idea

Please [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tem-ctrl/open-source-guru/issues).
