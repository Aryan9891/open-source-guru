---
title: Introduction
description: What open-source-guru is and what it can do for you.
---

open-source-guru helps you find, follow and keep up with open-source contributions on GitHub.

- **Follow repositories and labels**: get an email when an issue is opened with a label you follow
  (for example `help wanted`), or when that label is added to an existing issue.
- **Track your Open Source Contributions**: see your activity in open-source repositories, keep a
  streak going and set goals.
- **Share your profile**: a public page that summarizes your Open Source Contributions.

<Callout title="Work in progress">
  open-source-guru is under active development. These docs grow with each feature.
</Callout>
