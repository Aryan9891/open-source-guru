## Frequency

Each subscription has its own frequency:

| Frequency | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Right away** | An email within a few minutes. Matches that happen close together (within about five minutes) arrive in one email. |
| **Hourly digest** | At most one email per hour, grouped by repository. |
| **Daily digest** | One email a day, at your digest hour, grouped by repository. |

Your **time zone** and **digest hour** are in [Settings](/dashboard/settings). Daily digests are
sent during that hour, in your time zone.

Nothing matched? No email: we never send empty digests.

## What's in an email

For each matching issue: its repository, title, labels, why it matched (new issue or label added),
and whether it's already assigned. Every email links to your dashboard.

## Email addresses

Your **primary GitHub email** is added when you sign up, and can be used right away.

You can add other addresses in [Settings](/dashboard/settings), for example a work address. Each
new address gets a confirmation email, and **nothing else is sent to it until you click the
link**. The link is valid for 24 hours; you can resend it from Settings.

<Callout>
  For now, the dashboard sends every subscription to your first active address. To send a
  subscription to another address, use the [API](/docs/api): each subscription accepts a list of
  addresses, each with its own frequency.
</Callout>

An address can show as:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Active** | Emails are sent to it |
| **Waiting for confirmation** | The confirmation link hasn't been clicked yet |
| **Disabled** | Emails bounced, or you unsubscribed. Remove it and add it again to start over. |

## Unsubscribing

Every notification email gives you two ways out:

- **Stop emails for owner/repo**, under each repository: that address stops receiving that
  repository's notifications. Your other subscriptions keep going.
- **Unsubscribe from all notifications**, in the footer: nothing more is sent to that address.

Opening one of these links only shows what will stop; you confirm with a button. Email apps that
offer a built-in **Unsubscribe** button (Gmail, Apple Mail, …) unsubscribe from all notifications
to that address in one click.

To pause instead, [pause the subscription](/docs/subscriptions#pause-edit-or-delete).

## Motivation emails

Two optional emails, off by default. Turn them on in [Settings](/dashboard/settings) under
**Motivation emails**:

| Email | When |
|---|---|
| **Streak reminders** | Your streak needs a contribution: Friday evening for weekly streaks, every evening for daily ones (your time zone). See [Streaks](/docs/streaks#streak-reminders). |
| **Goal achieved** | You reach a [goal](/docs/goals). |

They go to your first active email address. Each one has a **Stop** link and supports your email
app's **Unsubscribe** button, which turn that email off in your settings.
