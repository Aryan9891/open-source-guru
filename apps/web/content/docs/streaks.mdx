title: Streaks
description: Weeks or days in a row with at least one Open Source Contribution.
---

Your streak counts the periods in a row with at least one
[Open Source Contribution](/docs/contributions).

## Weekly or daily

Choose in [Settings](/dashboard/settings), under **Streaks**:

| Streak | A period counts when it has at least one contribution |
|---|---|
| **Weekly** (default) | A week, Sunday to Saturday |
| **Daily** | A day |

Weekly is the default because open-source work is uneven: you wait for reviews, maintainers take
time to answer. Daily suits people who want a stronger habit.

Both streaks are always computed, so switching takes effect right away.

## How it's counted

- The **current week or day isn't counted as broken until it's over**: if your last contribution
  was last week, your weekly streak is still going until Saturday night.
- Your **longest** streak is kept, even when the current one ends.
- Days follow GitHub's graph: see
  [Which day a contribution counts for](/docs/contributions#which-day-a-contribution-counts-for).

## Streak reminders

Turn on **Streak reminders** in [Settings](/dashboard/settings) under **Motivation emails** to
get an email when your streak needs a contribution:

- **Weekly streaks:** on Friday evening, when the week has none yet.
- **Daily streaks:** in the evening, when the day has none yet.

Evenings are between 6 and 10 p.m. in your time zone. You only get a reminder when you have a
streak to keep, at most once per week or day, and only after a fresh refresh of your
contributions, so you aren't reminded about something you've already done.
