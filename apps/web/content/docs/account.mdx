## Settings

[Settings](/dashboard/settings) holds your time zone, your daily digest hour, and your
[email addresses](/docs/emails#email-addresses).

## What we store

- Your **GitHub profile**: id, login, name, avatar and primary email.
- Your **GitHub access token**, encrypted, used for sign-in only.
- Your **settings**, **email addresses**, **subscriptions** and the issues they matched.
- Your **contributions** as GitHub reports them (titles and links for public repositories; only a
  number for private ones), with your daily totals, streaks and goals.
- Your **API keys**, stored hashed: we can't read them back.

We only ask GitHub for read access to your email addresses. We don't access your repositories,
private or public, on your behalf. Contributions are read from GitHub's public data.

Your [public profile](/docs/profile) shows your Open Source Contributions, streak and public goals;
turn it off at any time in Settings.

## Signing out

Use **Sign out** at the bottom of the dashboard menu. API keys keep working: revoke them on the
[API keys](/dashboard/api-keys) page if needed.

## Deleting your account

At the bottom of [Settings](/dashboard/settings), **Delete account** removes your profile,
public profile, settings, email addresses, subscriptions, feed, contributions, streaks, goals and
API keys, right away and for good. You'll be asked to type your GitHub login to confirm, and
we'll send a confirmation email.

To also remove Open Source Guru from your GitHub account, revoke it in GitHub's
[Authorized GitHub Apps](https://github.com/settings/apps/authorizations) settings.

You can sign in again later: it creates a new, empty account.
